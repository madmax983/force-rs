**[Refactor Required Fields Collection]**
**Learning:** Re-implementing a filter, map, and collect chain into a `Vec` solely to check `.is_empty()` and then conditionally iterate it wastes memory with an intermediate allocation.
**Action:** When conditionally writing output based on the presence of elements, perform an initial check using `.clone().any(|_| true)` on a cloned iterator (or collect lazily) or filter first and evaluate if empty before collection to avoid unnecessary intermediate heap allocations. Avoid relying on anti-patterns just to skip `is_empty()`. Always ensure the replacement is cleaner, like filtering into a `Vec` and calling `.is_empty()` when the vector is eventually needed.
